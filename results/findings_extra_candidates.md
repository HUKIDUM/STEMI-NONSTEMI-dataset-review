## Derived from our 11 datasets (overlap: never treat as independent)

- **MIMIC-IV-ECG-ext-ICD** (https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-ecg-ext-icd-labels/1.0.1/): ICD-10-CM ED and hospital discharge codes for MIMIC-IV-ECG ECGs. Credentialed + DUA + CITI training. Same ECGs as `mimic_iv_ecg`. Its page does not mention STEMI, NSTEMI or I21 (read 2026-10-20).
- **PTB-XL+** (BG-UNCONFIRMED, page not read): a feature/label extension of PTB-XL, therefore the same ECGs as `ptb_xl`.

## Summary for planner

- The only open candidate found with explicit STEMI **and** NSTEMI patients is **wECGdb (#1)**. It is small (92 participants), comes from wrist / non-standard leads plus a 200 Hz 12-lead reference, and records ECGs within 24 h of PCI, which is not presentation.
- No open dataset was found with acute-presentation 12-lead ECGs, STEMI/NSTEMI labels and angiographic confirmation. "Not found" is based on 3 web searches and the pages listed above.
