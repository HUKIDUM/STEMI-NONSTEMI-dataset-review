## Conflicts / notes for planner

1. `recording_date` years 1984–2001 (OBS) vs "October 1989 – June 1996" (PN). Explained by the per-patient random date shift (PAPER, Methods > Preprocessing). Report only the documented period.
2. Paper percentages (second_opinion 0.62%, 73.7% validated) describe the first release; 1.0.3 observations are 2.55% and 73.65%.
3. PAPER Table 11 counts (e.g. Stadium I 186) are "cumulated from infarction_stadium and infarction_stadium2" on the first release. OBS on 1.0.3: Stadium I appears 166 times in stadium1 plus 19 in stadium2 = 185 (`results/ptb_xl_mi_by_stadium.csv`, ALL_ECGS rows).
4. Lead names in the headers are uppercase `AVR/AVL/AVF` (OBS); PN writes "AVL, AVR, AVF". The lead map must be case-insensitive (P4).
