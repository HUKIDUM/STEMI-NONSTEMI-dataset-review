## Notes for planner

1. Paper Table 2 counts are statement-level. They equal the OBS code+modifier pair counts (e.g. 165+330 = 4, 161+330 = 2, 160+330 = 1), so 1+2+4 = 7 acute MI statements on 7 distinct ECGs.
2. The meaning of modifiers 330–334 is from `code.csv` (OBS) and PAPER ("330–334 should be paired with statements in category M"). Whether "Acute" here means a clinically adjudicated acute MI is not stated; treat it as a report wording (L2).
3. `review_sph.py` takes 500 Hz from the documentation to turn N into seconds. The summary row still cites [MCR p.3]; PAPER Methods gives the same 500 Hz.
